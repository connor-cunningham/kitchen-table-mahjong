Privacy Policy

Kitchen Table Mahjong · Effective November 20, 2026

The short version

Kitchen Table Mahjong has no ads and does not track you. It collects only what is needed to seat you at a table with your friends: a first name you type and a random ID for your device. We do not sell or share your information.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your record of games, your Learn to Play progress, and any practice game in progress are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

What we store on our server

Nothing, unless you open or join a friends’ table. When you do, we store:

  • A guest identifier — a random ID created for your device. No email address, phone number or password is involved.
  • The first name you typed, shown only to the people at your table.
  • Gameplay information — your seat, the moves of each game, and chip totals for the table. Chips are a score counter with no monetary value.

If you use “Send us a note” (in the app or on our support page), we receive your message, any name or contact detail you choose to include, the app version, and whether you use an iPhone, an iPad or the web.

What we do not collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, advertising identifiers or browsing activity. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics software.

How information is used

Only to run the game: to seat you, show your name to your tablemates, keep the table’s score, and answer notes you send us.

Who processes it

Our server is hosted by Supabase (database and hosting) in the United States. Apple provides the App Store. Neither is permitted to use your information for its own purposes.

Keeping and deleting it

Open the app and choose Settings → Delete my online data to erase your guest identifier, name, seat and chip records from our server immediately. Tables that no longer have any players may be removed periodically. Notes you send are kept until they have been answered or are no longer needed.

Children

Kitchen Table Mahjong is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Use “Send us a note” in the app’s Settings, or the form on our support page.
