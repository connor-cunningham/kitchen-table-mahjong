Kitchen Table Mahjong

American mahjong with friends — for iPhone, iPad and the web.

Help

Is this the mahjong I play with my group?

Kitchen Table Mahjong is American mahjong: the Charleston, jokers, flowers, racks, and hands from a card. It uses its own original card of hands, the Kitchen Table Card, which you can open at any time during a game.

How do I play with my friends?

Tap Play with Friends, type your first name, and tap Open a new table. Text your friends the five-letter code. They tap Play with Friends and type the code. No account is needed.

Can I play on my computer?

Yes. Open Kitchen Table Mahjong on the web in your browser. If you would like your record, settings and saved game to be the same on your phone and your computer, choose Sign in and create a free account with the same email on both.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot your password? We email a six-number code to the address on your account; type it in and choose a new password. If the email doesn’t arrive within a few minutes, look in your junk folder.

The tiles are hard to read

In Settings → Tile look, choose High contrast or Large print. You can also turn on spoken tile names.

Are chips real money?

No. Chips only keep score. Nothing in the app can be bought, won or cashed out.

How do I delete my information?

In the app: Settings → Delete my online data. With an account: Account → Delete my account. See the privacy policy for details.
